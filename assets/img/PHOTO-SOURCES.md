Retrieved September 7, 2026. Publication context is not necessarily the date the shutter was pressed; none is labeled as the current 2026–27 membership.

Local copies are resized and JPEG-compressed for the website. Matching 640px copies in `marquee/` keep the landing strip lightweight. The source assets remain unchanged. Existing archival images were preserved but are no longer used on the active Aire pages. The separate Sol, Forma, and original Aire explorations retain their earlier images.
