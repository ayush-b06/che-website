The story behind the support

From our comunidad,
for our comunidad.

The CHE Educational Scholarship supports students pursuing health careers who want to give back to their communities.

Previously known as the Familia Paredes-Miramontes scholarship, its history includes the family’s campus taco fundraisers with CHE, which began in 2008.
